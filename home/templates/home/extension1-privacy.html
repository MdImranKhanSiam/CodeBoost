UGV Portal Customizer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

UGV Portal Customizer is a Chrome extension that personalizes the UGV student portal (ugv.edu.bd) by showing your name, photo, and department throughout the site. This policy explains what data the extension stores and how it is handled.

Data collected

  • Display name The first name you enter in the extension popup, used to label the nav button.
  • Profile photo An image you select from your device, stored locally as a base64 data URL.
  • Department name The department text you enter, used to update labels across the portal.

How it's stored

All data is stored exclusively on your own device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API. It never leaves your device.

What we don't do

No data is transmitted to any server or external service
No data is shared with or sold to any third party
No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind
The extension only activates on ugv.edu.bd

Deleting your data

You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the UGV Portal Customizer extension from Chrome, or by clearing extension storage via chrome://extensions → Details → Clear data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact Imran Khan via the website or open an issue on the extension's repository.
